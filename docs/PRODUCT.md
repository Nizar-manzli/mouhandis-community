# Product design: why people join, and why they stay

## 1. The problem
The diaspora wants to give back, and talented students at home need guidance. Informal mentoring fails for three reasons:
1. **No structure.** After two calls, nobody knows what the next step is.
2. **No accountability.** Relationships go quiet without anyone noticing.
3. **No reward.** Mentors get nothing visible for their time, and students have no proof of what they achieved.

Mouhandis turns the 2016 call ("one engineer abroad sponsors one student at home") into a system built around **clear commitments, KPIs and incentives tied to those KPIs**.

## 2. The journey (12 months)
| Phase | Months | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Onboarding | 1 | Sign the Pact, set 1–3 goals with key results, first sessions |
| Build | 2–6 | Skills, projects, applications; check-ins every 2 weeks |
| Launch | 7–12 | Opportunities, verified outcomes, endorsement, paying it forward |

## 3. The Pact
| Mentor | Student |
|---|---|
| 2 sessions a month | Prepare an agenda and attend |
| Reply within 48h | Check in every 14 days |
| Answer check-ins within 72h | Finish action items on time |
| Goals with measurable key results; verify outcomes honestly | Reply within 48h |
| Open doors when the student has earned it | Pay it forward at Ambassador level |

## 4. KPIs
**Mentor scorecard** (rolling 90 days): session cadence ≥ 2/month per mentee, median response ≤ 48h, check-ins answered within 72h ≥ 90%, mentee satisfaction ≥ 4.2/5, mentee key-result progress ≥ 60%, verified outcomes ≥ 1 per mentee per year.

**Student scorecard**: attendance ≥ 90%, check-ins on time ≥ 85%, own action items on time ≥ 80%, key-result progress ≥ 60%, engagement rated by the mentor ≥ 4/5, verified outcomes ≥ 1.

**Relationship health (0–100)** is a weighted blend:

| Signal | Weight |
|---|---|
| Session cadence vs. the Pact | 25% |
| Goal / key-result progress | 20% |
| Check-ins on time (late counts half) | 15% |
| Action items on time | 15% |
| Two-way satisfaction | 15% |
| Two-way responsiveness | 10% |

Design choices:
- **Missing signals are not punished.** Weights are re-normalised, and new pairs get a 14-day grace period.
- **Silence can't improve a score.** An unanswered message counts against the side that owes the reply, at its current age.
- **No goals after 3 weeks counts as 0.** Drifting pairs show up quickly.
- **Dormancy cap.** With no session for 30 days, health is capped at 40, which puts the pair on the admin's intervention list.
- Bands: Thriving ≥ 75 · Steady ≥ 55 · At risk ≥ 35 · Critical. Each band is shown with an icon and a label, never colour alone.

**Platform KPIs** (program team): approved mentors and countries, students waiting, request acceptance rate, median time to match, 90-day retention, completion rate, average health, health distribution, session satisfaction, mentoring hours, verified outcomes by type.

## 5. Incentives
Each reward is **gated by a KPI**, so the cheapest way to earn it is to show up and do the work.

| | Mentor gets | Student gets |
|---|---|---|
| Immediately | Badge, structured toolkit, verifiable CPD certificate | 1:1 world-class mentor, free |
| Healthy pair (≥ 55) | Committed candidates for their postings | Opportunities board unlocked |
| 90 days, ≥ 80% attendance | Can write an endorsement (+40) | Public endorsement |
| Points | Tiers: Supporter, Guide, **Champion (talent pipeline)**, Pillar, Nation Builder | Levels: Explorer, Builder (more opportunities), Achiever (visible in pipeline), Ambassador (peer mentor) |
| Outcomes | Public Impact Ledger, +100 per verified outcome | Verified outcomes on profile, +150 each |
| Completion | +250 and a certificate | +200 and a completion certificate |

The **talent pipeline** is the key business incentive for mentors. At Champion tier they see top students in healthy pairs, ranked by real KPIs, which is useful for hiring into their own companies.

## 6. Flywheel
Showing up keeps health green, which earns points, which unlocks rewards, which produces verified outcomes. Those outcomes become public proof that attracts more mentors and students.
